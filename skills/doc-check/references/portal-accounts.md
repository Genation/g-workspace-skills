# 14. Moving a client portal account to an existing contact

Section 14 of the doc-check skill, split out of SKILL.md; read when the table in SKILL.md section 1 says so.

When a client activates the client portal themselves (call it "client portal activation", never "sign up" or "register": the Genation account is managed by Genation Auth), the CRM gets an extra new contact, source `portal`. A returning client already had a contact: the owner or an admin moves the account to the existing contact, usually right in the app. The assistant only does this when staff ask, never suggests it while checking documents; needs the tool `genation-workspace_portal_accounts_link`, do exactly as the tool description says:
1. **Account:** the account's `id` in `genation-workspace_portal_accounts_list` (`contactId` = the self-activated contact the staff member names; find it with `genation-workspace_contacts_list` with `source` = `portal`, several matching contacts: ask).
2. **Existing contact:** ask staff which contact is the client's; staff must point to the exact contact. Do not guess or choose by name or email, even when only one contact has the same name; you may list search results for staff to choose from.
3. **Review item** (SKILL.md section 7), always, even when staff named both exactly: the move cannot be undone. Say exactly which account goes to which contact, with what tells the contacts apart, then end the turn: "Move the client portal account <email> from contact <temporary contact name> (<its email or phone>) to <existing contact name> (<its email or phone>)? The temporary contact will be archived, any pending invitation for <existing contact name> will be cancelled, and from their next visit the client will see the deals of <existing contact name>. This cannot be undone. Reply move to confirm."
4. **Move** only after the staff member types "move" in reply to that item, for exactly that account and that contact: `portal_accounts_link` with `id` = the account's id, `contactId` = the existing contact, `note` = the reason staff gave (if any). Any other answer (or the account or contact changed): do not move; a new pair needs its own review item. Errors: restate in plain words per the tool description, do not retry; `403 forbidden`: only the organisation's owner or an admin can do this.
